# Vowframe visual identity draft

The mark combines a cinematic frame with a restrained V monogram. It avoids wedding clichés so the identity can cover entrance films, love story films and travel-inspired concepts.

- `dist/assets/vowframe-logo-light.svg`: primary wordmark for dark photography and dark backgrounds.
- `dist/assets/vowframe-logo-dark.svg`: alternate for ivory and other light backgrounds.
- `dist/assets/vowframe-mark.svg`: square icon and browser favicon.

Palette: forest charcoal `#24312B`, ivory `#F7F4ED`, champagne `#E8D8B9`, warm bronze `#A98B64`. Keep clear space around the symbol at least equal to one quarter of its width. Keep the full wordmark at least 180 px wide on screen; use the standalone mark below that size.

This is a proposed logo. The name and mark should be checked for trademark and domain conflicts before public launch.
